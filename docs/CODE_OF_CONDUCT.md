# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Huddle a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone.

We welcome contributors regardless of their level of experience, background, nationality, gender identity, disability, or personal characteristics.

Our goal is to build a community where everyone can contribute, learn, and collaborate in a positive environment.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Providing helpful and respectful feedback.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences.
- Focusing on collaboration and the best interests of the project.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Personal attacks, insults, or threats.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexual harassment or inappropriate conduct.
- Any form of abusive or hostile behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Huddle community spaces, including:

- GitHub Issues.
- Pull Requests.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces where individuals represent Huddle or its community.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They may take appropriate action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Possible actions include:

1. A warning.
2. Temporary restrictions from community spaces.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Temporary or permanent exclusion from the project community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the private communication channels available in the repository.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as possible.

Please include relevant details and evidence whenever possible.
